How to read this

  • Paying subscriber: counted from their first payment to the end of the subscription. Free trials that never paid are not counted.
  • Churn: the membership lapsed for more than 30 days. Billing outages, where people kept listening while billing stopped, are not churn.
  • Renewal decision: every time a paid period ends, the subscriber either pays again or leaves.
  • Channel is the sign-up channel. KKBox anonymised the codes, so they are shown as numbers.

Data: KKBox, from the WSDM Cup 2018 churn challenge on Kaggle, 2015–2017. Only aggregates are published here. All rules and checks are in the data notes. Built with DuckDB, Python and Apache ECharts. By Artem Zadvoryanskiy.
